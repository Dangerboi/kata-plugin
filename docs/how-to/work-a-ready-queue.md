# Work a ready queue under the plugin

This page covers only what the plugin adds when a lead works through `kata ready`. Queue order and hand-off wording are not covered here.

Every host's plan text says it too: delegate by putting the work in the plan, not by creating child issues yourself (`kata create`) for subagents to claim. How the plugin binds and settles delegates is in the [oh-my-pi](../reference/oh-my-pi.md#delegates), [pi](../reference/pi.md), [OpenCode](../reference/opencode.md#delegates) and [Claude Code](../reference/claude-code.md#delegates) references.

- **Do not work session parents as tasks.** The labels `omp-session`, `pi-session`, `opencode-session` and `claude-session` show that a session made the issue as its plan parent ([metadata](../reference/metadata.md)), not who closes it now: a lead that bound it explicitly closes it itself, and an automatic close can fail ([closing rules](../reference/closing-rules.md)). Review and report a session parent still open after its children closed.
- **Skip plan tasks another session holds.** A plan task another lead session started or blocked stays that session's while it is open. Leave it unless the user says that session crashed or was killed, then take it over as your host allows ([closing rules](../reference/closing-rules.md)).
- **Review and resume a delegate's hand-back.** An issue a delegate hands back to you is labeled `needs-review`, and its `work.attention` may read `needs-human`. That flag is the plugin's, not a question for a person: read the delegate's report, then close the issue with your own evidence or delegate it again (on omp, clear the flag in your own shell first, as the [oh-my-pi reference](../reference/oh-my-pi.md#hooks) describes).
- **Name one item or ticket per brief.** A delegate's brief names the one plan item or ticket it works, and nothing that names a sibling; what counts as naming is in your host's reference. On pi, name a ticket by its qualified ref, because pi children do not bind ticket items, and a brief that names two items is not refused: the child runs untracked and closes neither ([pi reference](../reference/pi.md)).
