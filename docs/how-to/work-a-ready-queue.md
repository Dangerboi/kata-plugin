# Work a ready queue under the plugin

This page covers only what the plugin adds when a lead works through `kata ready`. Queue order and hand-off wording are not covered here.

Every host's plan text says it too: delegate by putting the work in the plan, not by creating child issues yourself (`kata create`) for subagents to claim. The plugin makes each plan item a child issue (or, for a ticket item, works that ticket), claims each delegated one and tells its delegate how to comment and close; the binding rules are in the [oh-my-pi](../reference/oh-my-pi.md#delegates), [pi](../reference/pi.md), [OpenCode](../reference/opencode.md#delegates) and [Claude Code](../reference/claude-code.md#delegates) references.

- **Do not work session parents as tasks.** The labels `omp-session`, `pi-session`, `opencode-session` and `claude-session` show that a session made the issue as its plan parent ([metadata](../reference/metadata.md)); they do not show who closes it. An automatic parent closes from its children's evidence, but one a lead later bound explicitly is that lead's to close, and an automatic close can also fail and leave it open with `needs-review` ([closing rules](../reference/closing-rules.md)). Review and report a session parent still open after its children closed; do not assume it will close itself.
- **Skip plan tasks another session holds.** A plan task another lead session started or blocked stays that session's (`todo.session`) while it is open, and ops on it from your session are refused. Leave it unless the user says that session crashed or was killed; only then take it over, with the user's `/todo takeover <task>` on omp or `takeover: true` elsewhere ([closing rules](../reference/closing-rules.md)).
- **Review and resume a delegate's hand-back.** A delegate that worked its issue and exited with it still open hands it back to you labeled `needs-review`, with a comment saying so, and usually with `work.attention` set to `needs-human` and a message such as `Subagent <name> completed without closing its issue; review its report and verification.` That flag is the plugin's, not a question for a person: read the delegate's report, then close the issue with your own evidence or delegate it again (on omp, clear the flag in your own shell first, as the [oh-my-pi reference](../reference/oh-my-pi.md#hooks) describes). Your accepted close of a plan task removes `needs-review`. A delegate that exited without running a tool hands nothing back: its assignment is rolled back.
- **Name one item or ticket per brief.** A delegate's brief names the one plan item or ticket it works, and nothing that names a sibling; what counts as naming is in your host's reference. On pi, a child binds a ticket item only when its brief names the ticket's qualified ref, and a brief that names two items is not refused: the child runs untracked and its report is posted on each of them, none closed ([pi reference](../reference/pi.md)).
